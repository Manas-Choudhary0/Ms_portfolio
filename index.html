ABOUT ME

Who Am I?

Just an 18-year-old from Jodhpur, Rajasthan, who gets genuinely excited when code does something unexpected — even when it crashes. I'm a first-year B.Tech CSE student at JECRC University and I'm absolutely obsessed with figuring out how things work under the hood.

I completed my schooling with 82% from a CBSE school in Jodhpur, and from day one I knew technology was my arena. Whether it's dissecting a sorting algorithm at 2 AM or exploring the latest AI model, I'm all in.

The Journey

I'm currently sharpening my skills in C, C++, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and modern AI tools. Every project I touch teaches me something new — I've broken more things than I've built, and I consider that a win.

I thrive on challenges, love competitive chess and badminton (yes, I have trophies), and find equal excitement in a clean algorithm and a roaring supercar engine.

The Goal

My mission is to become a skilled software developer who builds things that actually matter. I want to contribute to impactful projects, explore software engineering and AI opportunities, and one day — build something that changes how people experience technology.
